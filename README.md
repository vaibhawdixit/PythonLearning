Assignment 1
Task 1: Basic Mathematical Operations

Problem Statement:

A program that takes two numbers from the user and performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Expected Output

Enter first number: 10

Enter second number: 5

Addition: 10 + 5 = 15

Subtraction: 10 - 5 = 5

Multiplication: 10 * 5 = 50

Division: 10 / 5 = 2.0
